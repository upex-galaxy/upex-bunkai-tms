Write the PRD executive summary, as a senior product manager would.

**Input:**

- Business Model: [use .context/business/business-model.md]
- Market Context: [use .context/business/market-context.md]

Include:

1. **Problem Statement** (from the Business Model Canvas, refined)
   - The critical pain point the product solves
   - The impact on the current user

2. **Solution Overview** (what the MVP will build)
   - Core features in 3-5 bullets
   - How they solve the problem

3. **Success Metrics** (3-5 specific, measurable KPIs)
   - Adoption metrics
   - Engagement metrics
   - Business metrics

4. **Target Users** (2-3 short user personas)
   - Profile name
   - Short description
   - Main pain point

**Format:** structured Markdown, ready to copy into `.context/PRD/executive-summary.md`

**Constraints:**

- 2 pages at most
- Concise, actionable bullets
- KPIs that are measurable and realistic for an MVP
