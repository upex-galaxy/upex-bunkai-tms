# BK-213 — Workflow

> Jira field: `customfield_10104` · [View in Jira](https://jira.upexgalaxy.com/browse/BK-213)

After two weeks with notifications on, Elena notices her inbox drowns in run events from the nightly agent sweeps while the bug movements she truly cares about get buried. She opens Settings from the account menu, picks the Notifications section, and sees the grid. She switches run lifecycle in-app off but leaves its email on — the digest will still summarize them — and keeps bug lifecycle fully on. The change saves instantly. The next morning her bell shows only two notifications, both bug movements worth reading, and the run noise waits quietly in her daily digest email.
