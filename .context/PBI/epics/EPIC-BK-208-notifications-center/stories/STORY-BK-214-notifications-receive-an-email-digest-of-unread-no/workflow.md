# BK-214 — Workflow

> Jira field: `customfield_10104` · [View in Jira](https://jira.upexgalaxy.com/browse/BK-214)

Mateo's morning starts with email, not dashboards. At 8:00 the Bunkai digest is waiting: "7 unread notifications — Bunkai Web (5), Mobile App (2)". He scans the lines: three runs finished overnight (two passed, one failed), a bug moved to in progress, a new bug assigned to someone on his team. The failed run is the only thing that needs him. He clicks the open-inbox button, lands in the inbox, opens the failed run notification, and is looking at the run before his coffee cools. On quiet days no email arrives at all — which is exactly how he knows nothing needs him.
