# BK-508 — Workflow

> Jira field: `customfield_10104` · [View in Jira](https://jira.upexgalaxy.com/browse/BK-508)

A QA Lead who owns a workspace gets a data-portability request from legal and opens Settings. A ***Data export*** section is there — it is there because he is the Owner; the same section is simply absent for everyone else on the team.

He requests the export. The screen does not hang waiting for it: the request is acknowledged immediately and the section starts showing that an export is being prepared, along with when he asked for it. He can leave the page and come back.

When it is ready the section offers the archive as a single download, along with how long the download stays available. He downloads it and hands it on. If he comes back after that window has lapsed, the section does not present a dead link — it says the archive has expired and offers to prepare a new one.

If the export fails, he is told it failed and given a retry, rather than watching "preparing" forever. And if he asks for a second export while one is already being prepared, he is told why that is refused instead of quietly getting two.
