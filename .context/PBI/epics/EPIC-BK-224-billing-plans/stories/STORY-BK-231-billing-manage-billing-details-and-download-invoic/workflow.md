He opens Settings, then Billing, fixes the company name in the billing details card, and downloads the three PDFs from the invoice table — done before the meeting ends. Two months later the corporate card expires and the renewal fails: the Billing view greets him with an amber banner and a grace-period date instead of a surprise lockout. He swaps the card, hits retry, the banner clears, and nobody on the team ever notices.
