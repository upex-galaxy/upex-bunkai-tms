## Overview

## User story

As Mateo Silva (QA Lead / Quality Engineering Manager), I want to keep the company's billing information and payment method up to date and download past invoices, so that my finance team accepts the paperwork without back-and-forth.

## Context

Once a workspace pays, the billing relationship needs maintenance: company details that must appear on invoices, a payment method that expires and gets replaced, and an invoice trail finance asks for at closing time. This story adds those surfaces to the Billing section. Activates when a paid subscription exists (upgrade flow live).
