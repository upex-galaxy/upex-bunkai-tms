# BK-232 — Workflow

> Jira field: `customfield_10104` · [View in Jira](https://jira.upexgalaxy.com/browse/BK-232)

Elena is setting up regression coverage for a new product area. Creating the 9th project, an amber banner slides in: "Acme QA is approaching its project limit (9 of 10)". She dismisses it and keeps working. A week later the 11th project is one too many: instead of a cryptic failure she gets a clean modal — Free plan, 10 of 10 projects used — and, because she is not the payer, it tells her Mateo owns the workspace. She pings him on chat; he upgrades from his own Billing view; she hits retry on the still-open creation form and the project appears. No session reload, no mystery error, no support ticket.
