## Overview

## User story

As Mateo Silva (QA Lead / Quality Engineering Manager), I want to downgrade our plan or cancel the subscription myself, with the consequences spelled out before I confirm, so that changing our spend never risks the team's test data or requires a support ticket.

## Context

The exit half of self-serve billing, and the half that builds trust: a downgrade or cancellation must preview exactly what the workspace loses (limits, not data), take effect predictably (end of the paid period for cancel), and remain reversible (resubscribe before the period ends). Nothing is ever deleted — resources beyond the target plan's limits become read-only. Activates when a paid subscription exists (upgrade flow live).
