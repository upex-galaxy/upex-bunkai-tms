## Description

## What this epic is

This is the default intake container for defect-shaped work items (Bug / Defect) that have no natural feature epic to attach to. It is not a feature epic in any meaningful sense: it carries no coherent product scope, by design.

A sample of its 40 children confirms this: they span authentication, workspace switching, module creation, the ATC builder, the markdown editor, coverage and traceability, PAT/token issuance, and several unrelated staging deployment issues, with statuses ranging across Closed, Ready For QA, Duplicated, and REJECTED. A handful of children are not even Bug/Defect items — two are Improvements and one is a Story (BK-262) — filed here for the same reason: nothing else fit.

## What not to infer

Membership in this epic says nothing about which feature, module, or story a child affects. Do not use it for feature grouping, reporting, or triage routing. Each child's own summary, links, and any parent-story reference in its title are the source of truth for what it actually concerns.

## Where defects should go instead, when possible

When a defect can be traced to a specific feature story or epic, file or move it there instead of leaving it here. This epic exists precisely for the residual case where that is not possible or not yet known.
