## Overview

## User story

As Mateo Silva, QA Lead, I want to attach test plans to a milestone and see an aggregated readiness view, so that I know whether a release goal is on track and get a clear signal when it is overdue.

## Context

With plans reporting their own progress and milestones carrying target dates, this story connects the two: a milestone aggregates the progress of its attached plans into a single readiness figure. When the target date passes with readiness incomplete, the milestone is flagged overdue. This is the view Mateo brings to release go/no-go conversations. The story activates once its dependency epics, the milestone container, and plan progress tracking are live.
