## Overview

## User story

As Mateo Silva, QA Lead, I want to close a test plan recording a final verdict and an outcome summary, so that finished cycles become read-only history my team and auditors can trust as evidence.

## Context

A plan that stays open forever is a to-do list, not evidence. Closing a plan freezes it: the verdict, the outcome summary, and the progress at close time are recorded, and the plan becomes read-only from then on. This is the artifact Mateo hands to compliance when asked for the evidence trail of a release cycle. The story activates once its dependency epics and the plan container story are live.
