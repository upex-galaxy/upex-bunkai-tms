## Overview

## User story

As a Senior QA Engineer, I want to land on a personalized welcome banner when I open the workspace so that I immediately know who I am, which workspace I am in, and what changed since I was last active.

## Definition of done

- The banner appears every time a signed-in member opens Home.
- The change does not regress existing Home navigation or other screens.

## Technical notes

Renders into `home.jsx` (master-design-plan.md §4.2), Home's greeting header.
