# BK-264 — Scope

> Jira field: `customfield_10055` · [View in Jira](https://jira.upexgalaxy.com/browse/BK-264)

- Assign a defect to one workspace member at a time
- Reassign a defect to a different workspace member
- Unassign a defect, returning it to "no owner"
- See who a defect is currently assigned to wherever a defect is shown
- Move a defect forward through its lifecycle in this order: open, then in progress, then resolved, then closed
- Reject a status move that skips a stage or goes backward, with a clear reason shown to the member attempting it
- Reject an assignment to someone who is not an active workspace member, or whose role in the workspace is view-only, with a clear reason shown
- Record who performed each assignment and each status change
