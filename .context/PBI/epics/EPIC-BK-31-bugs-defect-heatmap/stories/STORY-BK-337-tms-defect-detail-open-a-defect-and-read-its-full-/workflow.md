# BK-337 — Workflow

> Jira field: `customfield_10104` · [View in Jira](https://jira.upexgalaxy.com/browse/BK-337)

1. Elena is looking at the defects list for a project and clicks a Bug id, or the linked Run reference on the same row.
2. The defect detail record opens, showing the defect's id, severity, status, title, full module path, and who filed it and when.
3. She reads the description, the numbered steps to reproduce (with the step that actually failed marked), and the Expected vs Actual boxes.
4. She opens an evidence row to review what was captured when the defect was filed.
5. If the defect came from a run, she follows the Origin links to the originating ATC or to the run itself, landing on the exact failing step.
6. If the defect was filed standalone, she instead sees a quiet notice that it has no run or ATC attached.
