# BK-507 — Workflow

> Jira field: `customfield_10104` · [View in Jira](https://jira.upexgalaxy.com/browse/BK-507)

A Senior QA Engineer opens a Project and looks at its ATC list after a module refactor has left thirty or forty ATCs sitting under the wrong Module. She ticks the rows she wants — or ticks the header control to take everything currently listed — and a count of the selection appears alongside a bulk-edit action that was not there a moment ago.

She picks the field she wants to change: move to a Module, add or remove a tag, or set the layer. Before anything is written she sees a confirmation that names the change and the count in plain words — "Move 40 ATCs to Checkout / Payments" — so the size of what she is about to do is never a surprise.

She confirms, and the list updates. When everything went through, the selection clears and the rows show their new values. When it did not, she gets an honest split: how many changed, how many did not, and the reason for each one that did not. The ATCs that changed keep their new values; the ones that failed stay selected, so her next action is a retry of just those, not a rerun of all forty.
