# BK-215 — Workflow

> Jira field: `customfield_10104` · [View in Jira](https://jira.upexgalaxy.com/browse/BK-215)

Elena is mid-run when staging stops responding. Instead of switching to Slack, she opens the chat panel from the app shell — it slides in next to her Run without leaving the page. She types "Is staging down? My run just stalled" and sends it. Sara, working in the same Workspace, sees the message pop into the channel instantly and answers "Yes — redeploying, 5 min". Elena glances at the roster to confirm Mateo is online too, drops a follow-up, and gets back to her Run. The next morning the whole exchange is still in the channel history, exactly where the work happened. When her train Wi-Fi cuts out and comes back, the channel quietly fills in the messages she missed — no refresh, no gap.
