## Overview

## User story

As Mateo Silva, a QA Lead, I want to search the message history across the channels I can access, so that decisions and context discussed in chat are retrievable when I need them — during planning, audits, or onboarding.

## Context

A chat that cannot be searched is a memory hole: the decision about why a Test was descoped lives three weeks up in a channel nobody will scroll. This story adds search across the member's accessible channels, with filters by channel, author, and date, and a jump-to-message action that lands in the conversation at the right spot. Search respects the same visibility rules as the channels themselves. It builds on the workspace and project channel stories and activates once messages exist to search.
