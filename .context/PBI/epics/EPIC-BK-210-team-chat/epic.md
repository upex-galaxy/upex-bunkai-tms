## Description

## Goal

QA work is conversational. When Elena needs to ask "is staging down?" in the middle of a Run, that conversation belongs where the Runs live — not in a Slack thread that loses every link back to the work. Team Chat brings real-time conversation into the Workspace so discussion stays attached to the Workspace and to the entities under test (ATCs, Tests, Runs), instead of losing context to external tools.

## Scope boundary

***In scope***: real-time channels at Workspace and Project level, @-mentions of teammates, entity rich links (ATC / Test / Run), editing and deleting your own messages, and searching the message history.

***Out of scope***: direct 1:1 messages, voice or video calls, and external guests. These are deliberate exclusions, not omissions.
