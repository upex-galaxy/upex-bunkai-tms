# BK-443 — Out Of Scope

> Jira field: `customfield_10101` · [View in Jira](https://jira.upexgalaxy.com/browse/BK-443)

- Sharing a saved view with a teammate, or any workspace-visible or team-owned view — saved views are private to the member who saved them in this story. Sharing is the natural follow-up and is deliberately deferred so this story stays the size of its siblings in this epic
- A default or pinned view that opens automatically when a surface loads
- Reopening the app to the last filter combination used, unnamed and automatic — this story saves views a person names on purpose
- Saved views on surfaces outside the four named in Scope
- Changing which filters any surface offers, or adding new filter dimensions
- Changing what the Traceability chain filter bar does — BK-48 shipped that filtering and the way it keeps filters in the page address, and this story revisits neither
- Persisting which tabs are open in the workbench — BK-147 owns open tabs, and this story does not extend it
- Rich chat links to a single entity (BK-218) — a different feature that resolves one entity, not a filtered view
- Exporting, scheduling, or emailing the contents of a saved view
